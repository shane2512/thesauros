01 — The problem

Idle treasuries earn nothing while payroll is a manual spreadsheet. Handing an LLM the wallet fixes the idleness and creates a worse problem: it can be talked into signing the wrong thing.

02 — The fence

Thesauros is that fix, done safely. Reasoning proposes; a pure, deterministic policy engine disposes. Every move needs a signed AllowReceipt from that engine, and you can freeze the treasury at any time. The power stays in your hands.
